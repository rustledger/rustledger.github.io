# Why rustfava?

rustfava brings the power of Fava's web interface to your desktop as a native application, powered by rustledger's blazing-fast backend.

## Key Benefits

### Native Performance

Built with Tauri instead of Electron, rustfava launches instantly and uses minimal resources. No waiting for a browser to spin up.

### Powered by rustledger

Under the hood, rustfava uses rustledger for parsing and validation. Your files are processed in milliseconds, not seconds.

### Truly Offline

Your financial data never leaves your machine. No cloud sync, no accounts to create, no internet required after installation.

### Familiar Interface

If you've used Fava before, you'll feel right at home. The same powerful web interface, now as a desktop app.

### Cross-Platform

One application that works on Linux, macOS, and Windows. Same experience everywhere.

## Who is it for?

- **Beancount/rustledger users** who want a native desktop experience
- **Privacy-conscious users** who want their financial data to stay local
- **Power users** who manage multiple ledger files
- **Anyone** who prefers desktop apps over web browsers
